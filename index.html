🎯 Core Objectives

  • Absolute Error: $\text{Absolute Error} = \vert{}\text{Measured} - \text{Actual}\vert{}$
  • Maximum Absolute Error: $\text{MAE} = \frac{1}{2} \times \text{Scale Interval}$
  • Limits & Ranges: $\text{Lower Limit} \le \text{Actual Value} < \text{Upper Limit}$
  • Relative Error: $\text{Relative Error} = \frac{\text{MAE}}{\text{Measured Value}}$
  • Percentage Error: $\text{Percentage Error} = \text{Relative Error} \times 100\%$
